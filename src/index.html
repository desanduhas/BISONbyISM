About the company

Building dependable materials for a growing industry.

Century Zone (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan construction-materials company and the owner of the BISON brand.

Under the BISON brand, the company manufactures and supplies essential construction products for modern building and finishing requirements. Its focus on quality, strength, reliability and consistent performance supports residential, commercial, industrial and general building projects.

Why BISON

Performance you can build on.

BISON block products have been subjected to technical testing through the Building Materials Research & Testing Division of the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO).

The company profile references a 2024 fly ash block test report against BS EN 771-3, including dimensions, crushing strength, water absorption due to capillary action and gross density.
